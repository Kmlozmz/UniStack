# Changelog

Notable changes for each published release, written for the people who use the app.

Each entry explains **what the user notices**, not which files were touched. "Fixed average calculation"
belongs here; "refactored data layer" does not. If something isn't noticeable in the app, it belongs
in git history instead.

Prior versions are archived in `docs/changelog-historico.md` outside the APK. The app only shows the
installed version's history.

## [1.0.2] — 2026-10-06

**Catching up on attendance.** When logging past unrecorded classes, you can now select directly
among all four statuses (attended, excused absence, unexcused absence, or cancelled), without having
to edit them afterwards.

**About and support.** Redesigned the about screen to explain with full clarity that your data stays
exclusively on your phone. Added a row to view the current version's release notes and direct access
to the Telegram support community.

**Updates and stability.** Fixed a freeze when opening the open-source licenses list and an issue
where the update installation button occasionally stayed locked. Also resolved an unexpected crash
when opening FAQs on devices set to English.

**Official repository channel.** This release connects future updates directly to UniStack's main
repository for faster checks and downloads.

## [1.0.1] — 2026-09-20

**Updates, redesigned.** The entire updates interface and workflow was redesigned, featuring a
three-dot menu and customizable preferences: Wi-Fi only, update notifications, and check frequency.

**Appearance, cleaner.** Removed redundant and cluttered controls in Shape, Components, and
Typography. Kept only what truly matters: corner roundness and density; Settings icons (colors and
shapes) and a pending-task counter in the bottom bar; font families in a live preview list with eight
new typefaces included—Inter, Manrope, DM Sans, Outfit, Space Grotesk, Nunito, Lora, and JetBrains
Mono.

**Your home, your way.** Five new customizable blocks for the Home screen: Courses, This Week,
Attendance, Weekly Expenses, and Pinned Notes, with reordering handles. Choose your default opening
tab.

**Grades and attendance.** You can now type term grades directly. Attendance check-ins now follow the
animation selected in Settings › Appearance › Motion.

**Flexible setup.** Academic period dates are now optional during initial onboarding, allowing
immediate use.

**Other.** Minor performance improvements and bug fixes.

## [1.0.0] — 2026-09-17

**Initial Release.** The first stable version of UniStack: your academic life in one single app.
Everything stays on your device, with no accounts and no ads.

**Courses and grades.** Log each grading term with its percentage. The app calculates your GPA with
evaluated work—never guessing scores—and predicts your best and worst-case outcomes.

**Your semester, from start to finish.** Create your academic term, mark holidays, and archive it
when it ends. Each completed semester is saved with its GPA and an exportable report card.

**Schedule and attendance.** Classes with days, hours, and rooms; an events calendar and attendance
tracking with absence warnings.

**Tasks & deadlines.** Due dates, subtasks, attachments (photos, audio, files), and graded deliverables.

**Expenses.** Log college expenses in seconds by category, set weekly or monthly budgets, and see
where your money goes.

**Quick notes and grade calculator.** Rich notes, checklists, photos, audio notes, and a calculator
to simulate required final grades.

**Your way.** Timely notifications, 28 themes, dark/light modes, accessibility settings, in English
and Spanish.

**Your data, always with you.** Complete offline backups restore all notes, photos, and audio
seamlessly to any phone.
