# UniStack Accessibility Checklist

Use this checklist whenever a screen adds controls or changes layout.

- Touch targets are at least 48dp for chips, icon buttons, cards with click handlers and bottom navigation.
- Selected controls expose visual state and semantic state when possible.
- Text does not depend on color alone to communicate status.
- Important actions have labels or content descriptions.
- Dialogs name the destructive action and provide cancel.
- Form fields show validation close to the field.
- Dynamic font does not overlap buttons, cards or bottom navigation.
- Empty states have one clear action and no long instructional copy.
- Tablet widths avoid stretched single-purpose cards when two columns improve scanning.
- Light, dark and system themes keep contrast readable.
